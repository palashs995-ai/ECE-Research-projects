# Wireless Signal Processing & 6G Communication Simulations  
### Palash Shrivastava

This repository contains MATLAB-based simulation projects focused on advanced topics in:

- Digital Signal Processing (DSP)
- Wireless Communication Systems
- Channel Modeling & Estimation
- Waveform Design for Next-Generation (6G) Systems
- Massive MIMO and Performance Analysis

These projects emphasize theoretical validation, channel modeling, and BER performance evaluation under realistic communication environments.

---

# 📡 Projects Included

---

## 1️. OFDM System Simulation & BER Analysis
**File:** `ofdm_ber_simulation.m`

- Designed baseband OFDM transceiver with cyclic prefix.
- Modeled Additive White Gaussian Noise (AWGN) channel.
- Evaluated Bit Error Rate (BER) vs Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR).
- Analyzed spectral efficiency and performance behavior.

Concepts Covered:
- IFFT/FFT processing
- OFDM modulation
- BER performance evaluation

---

## 2️. OFDM Channel Estimation under Rayleigh Fading
**File:** `OFDM_Channel_Estimation_Rayleigh_Fading.m`

- Modeled Rayleigh fading wireless channel.
- Implemented channel equalization.
- Evaluated OFDM performance under multipath fading.
- Studied impact of fading on BER degradation.

Concepts Covered:
- Rayleigh fading
- Wireless channel modeling
- Channel equalization
- Performance evaluation in fading environments

---

## 3️. OTFS vs OFDM Performance Comparison
**File:** `OTFS_vs_OFDM_Working.m`

- Compared conventional OFDM with simplified OTFS modulation.
- Evaluated performance under Rayleigh fading channel.
- Analyzed BER behavior under mobility-prone environments.

Concepts Covered:
- Time-frequency processing
- Delay-Doppler representation (conceptual OTFS)
- 6G waveform research direction
- Robustness under fading channels

---

## 4️. Massive MIMO BER Simulation
**File:** `Massive_MIMO_Working.m`

- Simulated multi-antenna communication system.
- Implemented Zero-Forcing (ZF) equalization.
- Evaluated BER performance with spatial diversity.
- Demonstrated impact of increasing receive antennas.

Concepts Covered:
- MIMO systems
- Massive MIMO (6G core concept)
- Linear detection techniques
- Spatial multiplexing

---

## 5️. Digital Modulation Performance Validation (BPSK)
**File:** `bpsk_ber_validation.m`

- Implemented BPSK modulation/demodulation.
- Compared simulated BER with theoretical results.
- Studied robustness under AWGN channel.

Concepts Covered:
- Baseband modulation
- Theoretical BER derivation
- Noise modeling

---

## 6️. Adaptive LMS Noise Cancellation
**File:** `lms_noise_cancellation.m`

- Implemented FIR-based adaptive filtering.
- Applied LMS algorithm for noise suppression.
- Evaluated time-domain performance improvement.

Concepts Covered:
- Adaptive filtering
- Least Mean Squares (LMS)
- Signal enhancement
